Why Decoy
Why do venue partners choose Decoy?

The market isn’t short of devices — it’s short of differentiation with substance. A device without R&D behind it, that can’t explain what it does, feels like a massage by the third visit.

Decoy is different. R&D, quality and compliance are overseen by Acheloy Biomed; the Active Ampoule formulas were developed by an R&D team with an NTU background; the Decoy device was developed by a PhD team from the Metal Industries Research & Development Centre (MIRDC) and is exclusively licensed to Acheloy Biomed; every session is recorded with the help of a non-invasive ultrasound device. These aren’t sales lines — they’re the real basis for how you explain the service, and the moat that is hardest to copy.

Self Assessment
Is your venue ready?
Before reaching out, consider the following. The more questions you answer "yes" to, the stronger the foundation for a successful partnership.
Q1
Does your venue already have a stable clientele, or are you looking for a differentiating service to attract new clients?
Both work — but the strategy differs. Stable clientele = direct conversion opportunity. Growing venue = a strong acquisition differentiator.
Q2
Is your target clientele quality-conscious, with spending power and an appreciation for science-backed services?
Decoy is a professional, accessible-premium beauty technology brand, suited to clients who value quality and will pay for trustworthy technology and clear guidance — not to price-led markets.
Q3
Is your team willing to undergo training and consistently deliver professional client consultations?
Decoy's value comes from professional consultation and data tracking. Operator quality is the key driver of rebooking.
Q4
Does your venue have a dedicated treatment space offering a private, comfortable care experience?
The Decoy experience is designed around privacy and comfort. A relatively private service space is recommended.
If you have questions about the above, or want to understand the implementation in more detail, reach out directly — there are no standard answers, only the right solution for your venue.

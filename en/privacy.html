Personal Data Collection Notice

Acheloy Biomed (岩寬生醫股份有限公司, “we”) operates the Decoy brand. Under Article 8 of Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act, we provide the following notice when you claim a Decode Card.

This English version is provided for reference. If there is any difference, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.

  1. Data controllerAcheloy Biomed (岩寬生醫股份有限公司). Contact: service@acheloy.com.
  2. PurposeTo create and send your Decode Card, to share information about Decoy services and events, and for anonymous statistics.
  3. Data collectedYour LINE user ID, your Care Rhythm Quiz result, your “My definition of beauty” text, and the time you claimed the card. We do not collect health, medical history, weight or similar data, and the quiz page itself stores no data.
  4. Period, region, recipients and method of use
    • Period: from the day you claim the card until you ask us to delete your data or this service ends.
    • Region: Taiwan. Data is stored on LINE and Google cloud services, whose servers may be located outside Taiwan.
    • Recipients: our company and Decoy store staff.
    • Method: creating your Decode Card, sending you relevant messages on LINE, and anonymous statistics. Your “My definition of beauty” text is never made public or used in advertising; if we would like to share it, we will ask for your consent separately.
  5. Your rightsAt any time you may ask to view your data, receive a copy, add to or correct it, stop its collection, processing or use, or have it deleted.
  6. If you choose not to provide dataYou can skip “My definition of beauty” and still receive your card. Without a LINE account, the Decode Card cannot be claimed.
Want your data deleted?Send 「刪除我的資料」 (“delete my data”) to Decoy on LINE, or email service@acheloy.com.

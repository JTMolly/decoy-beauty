Decoy is a non-invasive beauty technology brand. Both programmes use the Decoy device’s AirMist™ airflow misting, each with its own Active Ampoule; MIRROR also contains PeptiCarrier™ and uses the full Tri-Layer Delivery™.

Your Session

What a session is like

Assess → Plan → Care → Review. We tell you each step before it happens.

01
ASSESS
Assess

We talk about what you care about, your routine, your skin and your history, and check that today is a good day for it.

02
PLAN
Plan

What we’ll do, how large an area, how long, and why — all explained first. We tell you what may improve, and what the limits are.

03
CARE
Care

Using AirMist™ airflow misting, the Decoy device turns the serum into a fine mist that settles evenly over the skin — no needles, no wounds. You’ll hear the airflow and feel something like a light elastic flick; sensations vary, and you can pause at any time.

04
REVIEW
Review

Every session is recorded with the help of a non-invasive ultrasound device, and the record belongs to you. At the end of each stage we review together, then decide whether and how to continue.

After

Makeup, work and exercise are all fine afterwards. At home, keep skin hydrated, use sun protection and rest well — that’s the best way to carry it on.

Plan

Plans are built around your goal — for example 3 sessions over 4 weeks or 6 over 8 weeks — with a start, an end and a stage review. Whether to begin, and how, is always your call.

Mirror Active Ampoule

What’s in the MIRROR Active Ampoule

Used in Precision Body Delivery, with PeptiCarrier™ active peptide carrier. Built around caffeine, Camellia™ Alishan Jin Xuan complex and red algae extract, for firmness and protection.

Firm · Refreshed
Caffeine

Not the one in your morning cup. In skincare, caffeine is often used around the eyes and on the body to help skin look firmer and more awake. It is also an antioxidant that helps skin cope with everyday environmental stress.

Antioxidant · Protection
Camellia™ Alishan Jin Xuan Tea Complex
Camellia Sinensis Leaf/Stalk Extract · Epigallocatechol Gallate

Camellia™ pairs Alishan Jin Xuan (TTES No. 12) tea extract with EGCG. Low-temperature extraction keeps the catechins — well-studied antioxidants that help skin stand up to sun and pollution and stay calm and comfortable. The raw material has completed skin-irritation testing and a 50-person human patch test.

Protection
Malachite Extract

A copper-rich mineral extract. Copper is a trace element skin relies on for its own defences; in skincare it helps skin resist environmental stress.

Firm · Plump
Jania Rubens (Red Algae) Extract
Jania Rubens Extract

A red alga grown in a lab by a French supplier, so no wild seaweed is harvested. In skincare it helps skin feel firmer and plumper. It received a BSB Innovation Award for most innovative natural active.

Hydration · Nourishment
Natto Ferment Extract
Bacillus/Soybean Ferment Extract

Soybeans fermented by natto bacteria, rich in small peptides and amino acids that help hydrate and nourish skin.

Absorption · Conditioning
PeptiCarrier™ Active Peptide Carrier
sh-Decapeptide-5 SP (decapeptide)

A synthetic peptide with a sequence identical to one found in the human body. In the formula it acts like a guide, helping the active ingredients absorb well into skin, and it conditions skin in its own right.

Before You Come

Please tell us first if

We’ll assess together whether it’s suitable, or suggest waiting. Where needed, please check with your doctor first. Not available under 12; minors need a parent’s consent.

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • The area has a wound, redness, sunburn, dermatitis, eczema, folliculitis or active breakouts
  • You recently had injections, fillers, thread lifts or surgery in that area
  • You are under dermatological treatment, or have an immune-related condition or other medical history
  • You have reacted to skincare ingredients before
FAQ

Questions you might have

How do I choose between LIGHT and MIRROR?+
For hydration, glow and fine lines on the face, choose LIGHT. For firmer skin and smoother-looking contours on the body, choose MIRROR. Not sure? At your first visit we’ll understand where you are, then decide together.
What is Tri-Layer Delivery™?+
It is how the device and Active Ampoules work together: AirMist™ uses high-speed airflow to turn the serum into a fine mist; PeptiCarrier™, an active peptide carrier, helps the actives absorb into skin; and Precision Formula means LIGHT for the face or MIRROR for the body. MIRROR currently uses all three layers; LIGHT uses AirMist™ and its Precision Formula. No needles, no wounds.
Will it hurt?+
There are no needles and no wounds. Most people describe it as a light elastic flick; thinner or more sensitive areas may feel it more. Sensations vary, and you can ask us to adjust or pause at any time.
Do I need downtime? Can I wear makeup afterwards?+
No downtime — makeup, work and exercise are all fine afterwards. At home, keep skin hydrated, use sun protection and get some rest.
How long does a session take?+
LIGHT takes about 60 minutes, including cleansing, a mask and basic skincare. MIRROR takes about 40 minutes per session, covering an area about the size of your palm. We allow a little extra time on your first visit.
How many sessions will I need?+
It depends on your goal. We design stage-based plans with a clear start and end — for example 3 sessions over 4 weeks, or 6 over 8 weeks — and review together before deciding what’s next.
Is the Active Ampoule a medicine?+
No. Both Active Ampoules use common skincare ingredients and contain no medicinal ingredients. The full ingredient lists are on this page.
I have sensitive skin. Can I still come?+
Please let us know. We’ll look at your skin first and may try a small area before the full session. If your skin is currently red, inflamed or has dermatitis, we suggest waiting until it settles.
Can I come while pregnant or breastfeeding?+
We suggest waiting. If you have a specific need, please talk with us and with your doctor first.
Can I come during my period?+
Yes. If your abdomen tends to feel uncomfortable during your period, we suggest scheduling abdominal sessions afterwards.
Will you take photos? How are they used?+
Each session is recorded with photos and a non-invasive ultrasound device. The record belongs to you, and you can view it. We ask your consent before taking photos and before any other use; declining public use never affects your care.
How is Decoy different from medical aesthetics?+
Decoy is beauty care, not a medical treatment. We don’t use needles or put anything beneath the skin. If what you need calls for medical assessment, we’ll honestly suggest you see a doctor first.

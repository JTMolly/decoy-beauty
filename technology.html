迅So槍不用針，而是以三層精準導入技術，用氣流把活性安瓶裡的精華變成細緻的霧，均勻覆蓋在肌膚上。這一頁說明它怎麼運作、妳會感受到什麼，以及 Decoy 是什麼、不是什麼。Instead of needles, the Decoy device uses Tri-Layer Delivery™: airflow turns the serum into a fine mist that settles evenly over the skin. This page explains how it works, what you’ll feel, and what Decoy is — and isn’t.

Tri-Layer Delivery™
三層精準導入技術Tri-Layer Delivery™
用氣流，不用針。科技不是用來對抗身體，而是協助我們更精準地照顧它。Airflow, not needles. Technology shouldn’t fight the body — it should help us care for it more precisely.
Layer01
AirMist™ · 氣流霧化
加壓的高速氣流帶動活性安瓶裡的精華，在出口處化成細緻的霧，均勻覆蓋在肌膚上。全程不用針頭，也不會在皮膚上留下傷口。Pressurised high-speed airflow carries the serum and turns it into a fine mist that settles evenly over the skin. No needles at any point, and no wounds.

Layer02
PeptiCarrier™ · 活性胜肽載體
MIRROR 活性安瓶配方中的活性胜肽載體（sh-Decapeptide-5 SP），協助配方中的活性成分被肌膚吸收。LIGHT 不含這一層。施作時會聽到氣流聲，感覺像橡皮筋輕輕彈在皮膚上；感受因人而異，隨時可以請我們調整或暫停。An active peptide carrier in the MIRROR formula (sh-Decapeptide-5 SP) helps the actives absorb into skin. LIGHT does not include this layer. You’ll hear the airflow and feel something like a light elastic flick; sensations vary, and you can ask us to adjust or pause at any time.

Your Experience
妳會感受到什麼？What will you feel?
科學可以很複雜，但體驗只需要簡單。The science may be complex. The experience is simply this.
無針、無傷口No needles, no wounds
迅So槍以氣流傳導，沒有針頭、沒有切口。多數人形容像橡皮筋輕彈，感受因人而異。The device works with airflow — no needles, no incisions. Most people describe a light elastic flick; sensations vary.
做完就能回到生活Back to your day
不需要恢復期。上妝、上班、運動都可以；回家多保濕、注意防曬就好。No downtime. Makeup, work and exercise are all fine; just keep skin hydrated and protected from the sun.
每一次都有數據紀錄Every session on record
每次課程以非侵入式超音波儀協助數據化紀錄，紀錄屬於妳本人；階段結束時一起回顧，再決定下一步。Each session is recorded with the help of a non-invasive ultrasound device, and the record belongs to you. We review together at the end of each stage before deciding what’s next.

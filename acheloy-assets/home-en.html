WHAT WE DO

Three things we care about

01

Research

Every formula begins with research into its ingredients. Decoy’s active ampoules are developed by a team with a National Taiwan University research background, and every ingredient is disclosed. Our founder and team’s R&D has earned 3 patents, and the patented device is additionally licensed under 3 invention patents (all Taiwan patents).

02

Quality

Our products are made by qualified cosmetics manufacturers, with product registration and Product Information Files handled as the regulations require, so every product has a clear origin and record.

03

Honest advice

We explain how a product suits your skin and how to use it. Everyone’s experience is different, so we never exaggerate or make promises we cannot keep.

OUR BRAND

Decoy — our beauty service brand.

Our beauticians use the Decoy device to turn the serum in each active ampoule into a fine mist and apply it evenly onto the skin surface. It is needle-free and leaves no wounds. Every session is recorded, so you can follow your own care over time.

OUR NAME

What our name means

Name

Acheloy

Ache (pain)+Loy (to take away)

The name comes from Achelois, the moon goddess of Greek mythology. It means “She who washes away pain.”

OUR PROMISEWe find where people are suffering, and we take that pain away.

Your Session

一次課程，會經歷什麼What a session is like

ASSESS → PLAN → CARE → REVIEW。每一步都會先告訴妳。Assess → Plan → Care → Review. We tell you each step before it happens.

01
ASSESS
理解Assess

先聊聊妳在意的地方、作息、肌膚狀態和過去的保養經驗，確認今天適不適合。We talk about what you care about, your routine, your skin and your history, and check that today is a good day for it.

02
PLAN
說明Plan

今天做什麼、範圍多大、需要多久、為什麼這樣安排，都會先說清楚。可能的改善會說，限制也會說。What we’ll do, how large an area, how long, and why — all explained first. We tell you what may improve, and what the limits are.

03
CARE
導入Care

迅So槍以 AirMist™ 氣流霧化，把活性安瓶裡的精華化成細緻的霧，均勻覆蓋在肌膚上，全程無針、無傷口。妳會聽到氣流聲，感覺像橡皮筋輕輕彈在皮膚上；感受因人而異，隨時可以喊停。Using AirMist™ airflow misting, the Decoy device turns the serum into a fine mist that settles evenly over the skin — no needles, no wounds. You’ll hear the airflow and feel something like a light elastic flick; sensations vary, and you can pause at any time.

04
REVIEW
紀錄Review

每一次都以非侵入式超音波儀協助數據化紀錄，紀錄屬於妳本人。階段結束時一起回顧，再決定下一步要不要繼續、怎麼繼續。Every session is recorded with the help of a non-invasive ultrasound device, and the record belongs to you. At the end of each stage we review together, then decide whether and how to continue.

After

課後可以直接上妝、上班、運動。回家多保濕、注意防曬、早點睡，就是最好的延續。Makeup, work and exercise are all fine afterwards. At home, keep skin hydrated, use sun protection and rest well — that’s the best way to carry it on.

Plan

課程依妳的目標設計，例如 4 週 3 堂、8 週 6 堂，有起點、有終點、有階段回顧。要不要開始、怎麼安排，都由妳決定。Plans are built around your goal — for example 3 sessions over 4 weeks or 6 over 8 weeks — with a start, an end and a stage review. Whether to begin, and how, is always your call.

FAQ

妳可能想問的Questions you might have

LIGHT 和 MIRROR，我該怎麼選？How do I choose between LIGHT and MIRROR?+
臉部想要水潤、光澤、細紋的照顧，選 LIGHT 臉部精準導入；身體肌膚想更緊實、線條更柔順，選 MIRROR 身體精準導入。不確定也沒關係，第一次見面我們會先了解妳的狀態，再一起討論。For hydration, glow and fine lines on the face, choose LIGHT. For firmer skin and smoother-looking contours on the body, choose MIRROR. Not sure? At your first visit we’ll understand where you are, then decide together.
什麼是三層精準導入技術？What is Tri-Layer Delivery™?+
是迅So槍和活性安瓶一起運作的方式：第一層 AirMist™ 用高速氣流把精華化成細緻的霧；第二層 PeptiCarrier™ 活性胜肽載體，協助配方中的活性成分被肌膚吸收；第三層 Precision Formula，依臉部或身體使用 LIGHT 或 MIRROR 活性安瓶。目前 MIRROR 完整使用三層；LIGHT 使用第一層 AirMist™ 與第三層精準配方。全程無針、無傷口。It is how the device and Active Ampoules work together: AirMist™ uses high-speed airflow to turn the serum into a fine mist; PeptiCarrier™, an active peptide carrier, helps the actives absorb into skin; and Precision Formula means LIGHT for the face or MIRROR for the body. MIRROR currently uses all three layers; LIGHT uses AirMist™ and its Precision Formula. No needles, no wounds.
過程會痛嗎？Will it hurt?+
全程無針、無傷口。多數人形容像橡皮筋輕輕彈在皮膚上；皮膚較薄或較敏感的部位，感覺會明顯一些。感受因人而異，隨時可以請我們調整或暫停。There are no needles and no wounds. Most people describe it as a light elastic flick; thinner or more sensitive areas may feel it more. Sensations vary, and you can ask us to adjust or pause at any time.
做完需要休息嗎？可以上妝嗎？Do I need downtime? Can I wear makeup afterwards?+

一次要多久？How long does a session take?+
LIGHT 臉部約 60 分鐘，含清潔、面膜與基礎保養；MIRROR 身體每堂約 40 分鐘，一堂照顧約一個手掌大小的範圍。第一次會多留一點時間聊聊。LIGHT takes about 60 minutes, including cleansing, a mask and basic skincare. MIRROR takes about 40 minutes per session, covering an area about the size of your palm. We allow a little extra time on your first visit.
需要做幾次？How many sessions will I need?+
依妳的目標而定。我們會設計有起點、有終點的階段方案，例如 4 週 3 堂、8 週 6 堂；階段結束時一起回顧，再決定下一步。It depends on your goal. We design stage-based plans with a clear start and end — for example 3 sessions over 4 weeks, or 6 over 8 weeks — and review together before deciding what’s next.

敏感肌可以做嗎？I have sensitive skin. Can I still come?+
可以先告訴我們。我們會先看看肌膚狀態，必要時先在小範圍試用；若肌膚正處於泛紅、發炎或皮膚炎的狀態，建議先等肌膚穩定。Please let us know. We’ll look at your skin first and may try a small area before the full session. If your skin is currently red, inflamed or has dermatitis, we suggest waiting until it settles.
懷孕或哺乳可以做嗎？Can I come while pregnant or breastfeeding?+
建議先暫緩。若有特別的需求，請先和我們、也和妳的醫師討論。We suggest waiting. If you have a specific need, please talk with us and with your doctor first.
生理期可以做嗎？Can I come during my period?+
可以。如果生理期腹部容易不舒服，腹部的課程建議等結束後再安排。Yes. If your abdomen tends to feel uncomfortable during your period, we suggest scheduling abdominal sessions afterwards.
